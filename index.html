01/ about me

I'm Nabhan Lyric Chowdhury, a software developer, chess player, artist and writer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

What started as a curiosity about art and computers grew into a dream, and then a profession, across media content, writing and code. Chasing that vision sharpened me as a writer, illustrator, programmer and chess player. Today I channel that energy into coding projects and writing books.
