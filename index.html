Odyssey Design

A local-first desktop suite: documents, spreadsheets, decks and a multi-track video editor. The timeline compiles the whole project into one ffmpeg filter graph, so there are no intermediate renders. 55 effects, real cross-dissolves, two-pass stabilisation, sidechain audio ducking, ~180 tests that render real video.
